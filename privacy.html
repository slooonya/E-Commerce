Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our e-commerce store collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website or make a purchase. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account and contact details — such as your name, email address, and delivery address when you place an order or contact us.
  • Order information — products added to your cart, purchase history, and payment-related details processed by our payment partners.
  • Technical data — browser type, device information, IP address, and pages visited, collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Preferences — language, currency, and cookie consent choices stored in your browser.

2. Cookies

We use cookies and local storage to run the store and improve your experience. Essential cookies are required for core features such as shopping cart persistence and remembering your language or currency settings.

Non-essential cookies may be used for analytics and personalization. When you first visit our site, a cookie banner lets you Accept all cookies or Decline non-essential ones. Your choice is stored locally under the key cookie-consent with a value of accepted or rejected.

3. How we use your information

We use collected information to:

  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Improve website performance, security, and usability
  • Remember your preferences across visits
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with trusted service providers who help us operate the store — for example, payment processors, delivery partners, or hosting providers — only to the extent necessary to provide their services.

5. Data retention and security

We retain personal data only for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, though no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential cookies at any time by clearing site data in your browser
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

7. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at admin@gmail.com.
